Crop Disease & Late Detection

⚠️ Other Major Challenges Farmers Face

Beyond crop issues, Indian farmers face deep structural, financial, and environmental problems.

🏦
Lack of Credit & Loans
Small farmers struggle to get bank loans and often borrow from moneylenders at 30–60% interest, pushing them into debt traps.
🌩️
Extreme Weather & Climate Change
Unseasonal rain, hailstorms, droughts, and floods are increasing. Farmers have no early warning systems and suffer sudden total losses.
🌱
Soil Degradation
Overuse of chemical fertilizers and pesticides has degraded topsoil across India. Soil loses fertility over years, reducing yields despite more inputs.
📦
Poor Storage & Post-Harvest Loss
India loses 15–18% of food due to lack of cold storage near farms. Perishables rot before they reach the market, especially in rural areas.
📶
No Digital Access or Tech Literacy
Many farmers, especially elderly ones, have no smartphones or internet. Even when government schemes exist, farmers don't know how to access them.
👩‍🌾
Labour Shortage in Rural Areas
Young people are migrating to cities, leaving an aging farming population. Harvesting and sowing seasons face severe labour shortages, raising costs.
💊
Overuse of Pesticides
Without proper guidance, farmers spray excessive pesticides. This harms soil health, pollutes water bodies, and causes serious health risks to the farmers themselves.
📋
Lack of Government Scheme Awareness
PM-KISAN, crop insurance (PMFBY), soil health cards — most farmers are unaware of these benefits or face heavy paperwork to claim them.
